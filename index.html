FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What buyers usually ask

Clear answers before you choose a machine or placement plan.

What products can these machines sell? +

They can support many packaged items that fit safely on the shelves, including bottled and canned drinks, snacks, yogurt, prepared meals, frozen foods in compatible freezer models and selected everyday essentials.

How does customer payment work? +

The customer taps or swipes an accepted payment method, opens the door and takes products. When the door closes, the system identifies the selected items and processes the transaction.

Can the shelves and product mix be changed? +

Yes. Shelf spacing and product selection can be adjusted to suit the location. Final compatibility depends on package size, weight and temperature requirements.

How do operators know what to restock? +

Connected management tools can provide inventory and sales visibility, helping operators identify low-stock items and best sellers before a restocking visit.

Which machine should I choose? +

The right model depends on available floor space, expected traffic, product type and how often the location can be serviced. Send us those details and we will narrow down the options.

Do I have to pay for anything? +

No. There is no cost to your business or property. We take care of installation, product selection, restocking, inventory management, cleaning, maintenance and ongoing service. You provide the space, and we handle the rest.
